## Passed

- **Start-up:** the first `starting` line names 0.1.15 and `2f8f3bc`; no error lines after
  it. Warnings once each: FIRMS needs a key; the Iowa camera pack has a duplicate id; eight
  WSDOT rows have no coordinates.
- **Graphics:** Automatic (balanced), the hint names the Radeon 740M.
- **Home view:** Home flies to the saved view (30.26° N 130.61° E, 256 km).
- **Styles and keys:** V and Shift+V through the five styles on the globe and in 2D; H; N
  (night measured on the screenshots: the US 30 % darker, South America 36 %, China and
  Australia, in daylight, unchanged); O turns 5° a second and a click stops it; C.
- **2D/3D:** the centre and tilt carry over (the HUD's −70° in 2D is the map's real 20° tilt).
- **Search:** "ISS" offers the object above places; PHNL, EDDF, coordinates; a callsign in
  full selects the aircraft.
- **Aircraft:** panel (LH 9MN, Frankfurt → Athens, flown and to go, Airbus A321, class,
  registration), dashed route, 3D models below 50 km at Frankfurt with both models' CC BY
  4.0 credits in the credit line; F follows at −35°.
- **Ships:** Digitraffic in the Gulf of Finland, the panel's "as broadcast" fields and flag
  from the MMSI, the Fintraffic credit.
- **Earthquakes:** magnitude type spelled out (mb, body-wave), depth, "No tsunami flag", USGS
  event page, freshness and confidence classes.
- **Weather:** radar over Chicago, clouds, NWS alerts, the legend listing only what is on the
  map; NHC cones and tracks, GDACS cyclones.
- **Cameras:** an Illinois frame with its source and CC BY-SA 2.0 terms, "stills only".
- **Layer rail:** each category opens to its switches; AISStream "needs key" opens its Source
  health entry with the credential field.
- **Collections:** Collect, Add map centre, export to a file and import it back.
- **Watch zones:** a 50 km circle at the map centre; event types offer only what this
  install can raise, with the reason for the rest; "Something enters the zone" raised
  "aircraft AUA21V entered" within a minute.
- **Timeline:** pause stops the clock; scrubbing back reads HISTORICAL; 60× plays about a
  minute a second (REPLAY); LIVE returns.
- **Settings and Diagnostics:** history stable (satellites 803 MB, 804 MB after the dedupe
  fix); updates on both channels ("no stable release yet"; with pre-releases, 0.1.15 is the
  latest); Diagnostics' sections; an export with no user path, no secret and no findings.
- **Accessibility:** text scale 150 % scales the shell, HUD and legend; Tab moves a visible
  focus ring through the rail; reduced motion takes Orbit out of the palette.

## Checked and not a defect

- The infrared clouds show their pixels as soft steps at 450 km over the Congo: the Meteosat
  tiles stop at 2.4 km a pixel, about the instrument's 3 km (now in KNOWN-LIMITATIONS).
- The weather layers off and a switch to 2D at 05:20–05:22Z: the operator was using the
  laptop.
- Exported collections end `.json`; the dialog hides the extension.

## Not walked here

What the checklist leaves to the operator (a clean-PC install, the offline section, a
desktop notification, Escape, which the remote session cannot press, Task Manager, comparisons

## The operator's settings

The laptop's layer switches and settings were put back as they were found: Space, Maritime,
Transportation, Environment and the imagery overlays off, text scale 100 %, reduced motion off.
The test watch zone round Frankfurt and the "QA check" location added in this pass were removed.
